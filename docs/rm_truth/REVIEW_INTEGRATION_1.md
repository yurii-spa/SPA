## Verdict: **CHANGES_REQUIRED**

There are no P0 findings on money or safety. RiskPolicy, the kill-switch thresholds, tiers, execution imports, LLM use, secrets and `landing/**` are all untouched. However, **five P1 regressions were introduced**:

- a Telegram spam regression in agent_health;
- owner decisions hidden in Director;
- a red CI ratchet;
- a public-number change on `/dashboard` and the homepage, made through the API and not authorised by the owner;
- the option grammar mis-scoping multi-step cards into whole-card buttons.

All five must be fixed (or the public one explicitly authorised by the owner) before the merge.

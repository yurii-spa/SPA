## (B) Two-dimensional risk (protocol × strategy mechanic)

**Verdict: not represented. Protocol risk and strategy-mechanic risk are collapsed into one label (tier T1/T2/T3) and one score keyed by adapter. Where a mechanic attribute exists, it is hard-coded rather than measured (WRONG).**

Exact structures:
1. **`data/adapter_registry.json` `adapters[key]`** = `{tier:int, protocol, chain, fallback_apy, research_only, per_protocol_cap, status, mp}`. There is no mechanic or strategy-type field. RiskPolicy caps are tier-indexed only.
2. **`ADAPTER_REGISTRY`** (`spa_core/adapters/__init__.py`, 36 entries) = tuples `(key, tier_str, AdapterClass)`. Again only tier.
3. **`data/protocol_risk_map.json` / `risk/protocol_risk_map.py`** = `{key: {tier, risk_score∈[0,1], note}}`, `tier_bands T1<0.25 / T2 0.25–0.6 / T3>0.6` (generated 2026-06-25, not regenerated). The mechanic appears only as free text inside one score. `maple` "private credit / undercollateralised RWA" 0.45 T2, `aerodrome_base` "AMM stable LP + AERO emissions" 0.45 T2, `ethena_susde` "delta-neutral, depeg & funding" 0.40 T2: three different mechanics, one dimension. Its only reader is `investment_os/agents/protocol_risk.py:36` (advisory).
4. **`risk/scoring_engine.py`** has subscore `yield_source_type` (real_cashflow > rwa > basis > emissions > points; `:22,168`). This is the closest thing to a mechanic dimension, but it is averaged into one protocol grade. It is offline bootstrap, and live data shows all grades = B.
5. **`paper_trading/strategy_registry.py:52-67` `StrategySpec`** = `{id, name, allocations{protocol_key: frac}, tier: "T1"|"T1+T2"|"T2"|"T3", target_apy_*, kill_drawdown_pct, status, gate_condition, strategy_class}`. **Strategy risk is expressed with the protocol-tier vocabulary** (`tier="T1+T2"`), so the mechanic risk of, e.g., Pendle YT (leveraged) is represented as "T3", the same label as a T3 protocol.
6. **Engine sleeves** `risk/policy_hy.py` (Carry, `hy_v1.0`) and `risk/policy_lp.py` (LP, `lp_v1.0`) are per-mechanic policies, run by loaded agents `com.spa.hy_cycle` / `com.spa.lp_cycle` (advisory books). **WRONG:** `paper_trading/sleeve_book.py:311-312` stamps every opened position `is_delta_neutral: True`, and `lp_cycle.check_positions_delta_neutral` (`:123-131`) treats a missing field as True (fail-open). Live `data/lp_paper_trading.json` (08:55:42 local) "LP/Liquidity" engine holds **maple $50,135 + fluid_fusdc $50,135**, both `is_delta_neutral: true`. These are lending/credit, not LP. `data/hy_paper_trading.json` "Carry" holds maple, fluid_fusdc, susde, morpho_steakhouse, all "delta_neutral". The engine label (mechanic) is not checked against the asset at all.
7. `risk/capital_policy.py` (engine weights A 60 / B 25 / C 15) and `risk/aggregator.py` have **no non-test callers**. `risk/strategy_stress_ranking.py` has none either.

Consequence: same-protocol, different-mechanic exposures (Aave supply vs Aave loop `strategies/s21_aave_loop.py` / `emode_looping.py`) would share Aave's T1 cap and score. No cap per mechanic exists. A future loop/LP sleeve would inherit protocol T1 caps.

Minimal fix shape: add `mechanic ∈ {supply, rwa_credit, lp, basis, loop, pt_fixed, yt}` plus a mechanic risk score to the registry. Cap per mechanic in RiskPolicy (a new ADR; the v1.0 freeze applies). Derive `is_delta_neutral` from the mechanic and fail-closed when it is missing.
